**2. Implemented: 26–200 GeV muon calibration; high-pT prescription and closure remain open**

The audited version passed raw `events.Muon` directly into selection. The 12 September implementation adds [`muon_ss.py`](../src/vcb/processors/muon_ss.py), called by [`vcbSkimmer.py`](../src/vcb/processors/vcbSkimmer.py) before `objects.good_muons`. It corrects ordinary NanoAOD momentum, preserves raw/scaled/nominal pT and object identity, and writes total and statistical/systematic component shifts with coverage status. Data and MC receive their scale correction to the generator reference; MC additionally receives residual resolution smearing. [`--muon-pt-variation all`](cli.md#muon-momentum-passes) produces nominal and all four total shifted skims independently from NanoAOD, recalculating selection, trigger choice, jet cleaning, matching and efficiency weights. It is separate from weight-only `--save-systematics`.

The same official MUO campaign used for the efficiency SFs includes `muon_scalesmearing.json.gz`. Its [2026-04-28 release notes](/cvmfs/cms-griddata.cern.ch/cat/metadata/MUO/Run3-24CDEReprocessingFGHIPrompt-Summer24-NanoAODv15/2026-04-28/changes.md) document a correction to the previous release and a separate VXBS variant. That corrected payload is still content-identical to `latest`. The official [MUO README](/cvmfs/cms.cern.ch/rsync/cms-nanoAOD/jsonpog-integration/POG/MUO/README.md) and [Coffea example](/cvmfs/cms.cern.ch/rsync/cms-nanoAOD/jsonpog-integration/examples/muoScaleAndSmearingCoffeaExample.py) specify scale plus resolution correction on MC, with corresponding variations.

The user-supplied [muon momentum PDF](/isilon/export/home/jhuan166/.codex/attachments/60aed387-817d-491a-9706-d09a9579a130/muon-momentum-scale-and-reso-corrections.pdf), pages 1–2, now confirms the application guidance directly: the corrections apply to tight, isolated muons in **26–200 GeV**. The preferred scale procedure corrects both data and MC to the generator reference; an alternative MC-to-data formula is supplied when data are left unchanged. The scale uncertainty must include both data and MC parameter uncertainties, while resolution uncertainty is treated separately and uncorrelated with scale. This is the medium-pT convention now implemented. It does not establish an extrapolation below 26 GeV or the separate high-pT prescription.

An illustrative evaluation of **the scale term alone** on 34,321 selected trigger muons with input pT between 26 and 200 GeV gives median absolute pT shift **0.028 GeV**, 95th percentile **0.170 GeV**, and maximum **2.59 GeV**. These are not a full smearing or acceptance study. Small average shifts do not establish irrelevance to a charge-sensitive analysis or threshold migration.

The bundled ordinary-momentum payload is used consistently with `Muon_pt`. No VXBS or TuneP switch is made. The nominal and varied corrections run before trigger choice and SFs. Lepton-to-MET propagation is not applied, an optional choice under the supplied JMAR guidance; PF relative isolation and physical charge retain their NanoAOD definitions.

The subsequently supplied [MUO efficiency recommendations](/isilon/export/home/jhuan166/.codex/attachments/3c9a6841-eca9-4a35-8cca-93ee9459f8b6/corrections.md:5) resolve the earlier **SF-regime concern**: choose efficiency corrections for the regime containing the bulk of the analysis muons. They explicitly discourage interpreting the regime boundaries as an instruction to switch SF sets event by event. The presence of **330 selected muons above 200 GeV** out of 34,651 therefore does not, by itself, establish incorrect use of `muon_Z` in this predominantly medium-pT analysis.
